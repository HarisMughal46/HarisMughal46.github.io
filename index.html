About me

From testing software to building it.

I'm a Computer Science graduate (BSCS, Government College University Lahore, 2022 – 2026, CGPA 3.50/4.00) based in Lahore, Pakistan. I'm currently a .NET Developer Intern at Synavos Global, where I design and maintain SAP Business One web portals and work with ERP data across SAP HANA and SQL Server, plus UDF, UDT and UDO customization. I've also recently started working on SAP Business One add-ons.

My path has run across the stack. I started in quality assurance as an SQA Intern at Systems Limited (functional, UI, smoke, exploratory and regression testing, with defect tracking in JIRA, API testing in Postman and browser automation in Playwright), had a short remote frontend internship at RelyingTech building with React, and now work on backend, .NET and ERP systems.

Outside work I build AI-native side projects such as Projexa, a multi-tenant project management platform, and Lumen, a multi-model AI chat app. The QA habit stays with me: most of my projects ship with automated tests and CI.
