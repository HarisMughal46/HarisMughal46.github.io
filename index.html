About me

From testing software to building it.

I'm a Computer Science graduate (BSCS, Government College University Lahore, Dec 2022 – Oct 2026) based in Lahore, Pakistan. I'm currently a .NET Developer Intern at Synavos Global, where I work on ERP development and SAP Business One customization: UDFs, UDTs and UDOs, plus SAP HANA and SQL Server queries.

My path has run across the stack. I started in quality assurance as a Software QA Tester / SQA Specialist at Systems Limited (3 months of manual and automation testing with JIRA, Selenium, Postman and Cypress), moved into frontend as a Software Developer Intern at RelyingTech (2 months of React and Next.js), and now work on backend, .NET and ERP systems.

Outside work I build AI-native side projects such as Projexa, a multi-tenant project management platform, and Lumen, a multi-model AI chat app. The QA habit stays with me: most of my projects ship with automated tests and CI.
